FAQ

Before you ask.

How is this different from a managed IT provider?

Most MSPs price by seat and don't touch POS systems or AI. This covers the network, the systems, the POS, and the AI layer under one flat-fee audit — no per-seat contract required to start.

How is this different from an AI consultant?

AI recommendations are worthless if the network is unreliable or the systems underneath are a mess. The audit looks at the whole stack first, then places AI where it actually fits — not as the whole pitch.

What if I only need one thing fixed?

Start with the $85 quick-fix call. If it's bigger than 20 minutes can solve, it gets scoped as a project from there.

Do you offer free consultations?

A short call to confirm fit, yes. Diagnosing or solving a real problem is what the paid audit and quick-fix calls are for — that's by design, so the time going into your business is time you're paying for, not whatever's left over.
